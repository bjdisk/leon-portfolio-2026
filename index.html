【挑戰 // THE CONSTRAINT】 在高度受限的預算條件下，必須打破單一場域限制，籌辦跨時程、多線併行的全台北汎德展間新車上市，同時克服並行活動所引發的客戶名單割裂與即時追蹤瓶頸。[ THE CONSTRAINT ] Under a severely limited budget, the single-venue constraint had to be broken — staging a multi-timeline, multi-track new-car launch across all Pan Germany dealerships in Taipei, while overcoming the lead-list fragmentation and real-time tracking bottlenecks created by parallel events.

【戰略與行動 // THE STRATEGY】 以商業回報為終點逆向規劃。大膽引入外部品牌贊助以極大化槓桿商業資源；首夜以「濱江發表晚會」定調品牌高度，次日迅速連動其餘三處展示中心推行「邀請制發表活動」，形成高密度的轉換矩陣。同步設計目標導向的動態數據回報流，理順跨展間的數據痛點。[ THE STRATEGY ] Reverse-engineered from the commercial outcome. External brand sponsorship was brought in to maximize resource leverage; opening night was anchored with the Binjiang Gala to set the brand altitude, then the remaining three dealerships activated the next day via invitation-only launches — forming a dense conversion matrix. A goal-anchored real-time reporting flow was built in parallel to resolve cross-venue data friction.

【影響與變現 // THE IMPACT】 精準收斂 128 筆高資產 VIP 的 CRM 動態，在活動結束後的一週黃金期內落實高效追蹤，最終將實體活動成功變現為 21 台全新車輛訂單。對比上一代 X3 上市一個月內統計，銷售增長約 10%。[ THE IMPACT ] 128 high-net-worth VIP CRM signals were precisely converged and tracked through the post-event golden week — converting into 21 new vehicle orders, a ~10% sales lift over the prior-generation X3 within its first month.

【挑戰 // THE CONSTRAINT】 於南港 Citylink 高密度公共場域進行品牌異業合作。面對極其混亂的實體車流與高密度人群動線，必須在極度緊湊的時間窗口內，交付零延遲、高規格的星光大道接駁與現場試駕服務。[ THE CONSTRAINT ] A cross-industry brand collaboration staged within the dense public arena of Nangang Citylink. Against chaotic vehicle flow and high-density crowd lines, the program had to deliver zero-delay, premium-grade red-carpet transfers and on-site test drives within an extremely tight window.

【戰略與行動 // THE STRATEGY】 將現場物流拆解為精密幾何。調度 4 輛專屬車輛，其中 1 輛設定為「特遣專車」，專對專配置給 Twitch 高層與核心合作廠商夥伴，在核心發表期間強力護航頂級利害關係人的極致體驗；其餘 3 輛車則實施高頻率、標準化的循環運轉動線。[ THE STRATEGY ] On-site logistics was broken down into precise geometry. Four dedicated vehicles were deployed: one designated as the "VVIP shuttle" — exclusively assigned to Twitch leadership and core partner executives during the prime window, escorting top stakeholders with an unbroken experience; the other three ran on a high-frequency, standardized loop.

【影響與變現 // THE IMPACT】 在 2 小時的極限壓力下，流暢交付 80 組以上頂級實況主與貴賓的接駁訪問。寫下單車每小時運轉 10 趟的極限效率，將高風險現場轉化為毫無摩擦的品牌完美觸達。[ THE IMPACT ] Within a two-hour pressure window, 80+ premier streamer and VIP transfers were delivered seamlessly. Each vehicle hit a ceiling of 10 trips per hour — converting a high-risk public arena into a frictionless brand touchpoint.

【挑戰 // THE CONSTRAINT】 於麗寶國際賽車場，統籌全日流程極其緊湊、具備高度物理風險的專業賽道體驗。現場必須萬無一失地掌控 36 台高規格性能車款與 3 位專業賽車教練的運轉節奏。[ THE CONSTRAINT ] At Lihpao International Circuit, orchestrating a full-day program of tightly-packed timing and high physical risk. On-site command had to flawlessly hold the cadence of 36 high-performance vehicles and 3 professional racing coaches.

【戰略與行動 // THE STRATEGY】 面對現場大客流引發的 Pit 區動線實體擁堵，迅速重組現場資源進行動態分流，確保奢華體驗不中斷。活動結束後，拒絕流於表面的結案，主動將現場應變提煉為結構化的「流程寬裕化（Buffer-time allocation）」協議，將動態經驗制度化，落實未來的風險預判。[ THE STRATEGY ] When peak guest flow physically blocked the Pit lane, on-site resources were instantly redeployed to redirect movement and keep the luxury experience uninterrupted. Refusing a surface-level wrap-up, the live response was crystallized into a structured "Buffer-Time Allocation" protocol — turning improvisation into institutional risk foresight.

【影響與變現 // THE IMPACT】 不僅寫下 97.2% 的頂級車主高出席率，更達成零意外安全交付，成功將個人的現場調度力，轉化為可複製、可留存的企業制度資產。[ THE IMPACT ] Beyond delivering a 97.2% premium-owner attendance rate, the program closed with zero incidents — successfully converting personal field-command into a replicable, durable institutional asset.
