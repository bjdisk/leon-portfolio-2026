【挑戰 // THE CONSTRAINT】 於南港 Citylink 高密度公共場域進行品牌異業合作。面對極其混亂的實體車流與高密度人群動線，必須在極度緊湊的時間窗口內，交付高效率、高規格的星光大道接駁與現場試駕服務。[ THE CONSTRAINT ] A cross-industry brand collaboration staged within the dense public arena of Nangang Citylink. Against chaotic vehicle flow and high-density crowd lines, the program had to deliver zero-delay, premium-grade red-carpet transfers and on-site test drives within an extremely tight window.

【戰略與行動 // THE STRATEGY】 將現場物流拆解為精密幾何。調度 4 輛專屬車輛，其中 1 輛設定為「特遣專車」，專對專配置給 Twitch 高層與核心合作廠商夥伴，在核心發表期間確保頂級利害關係人的全程體驗不受干擾；其餘 3 輛車則實施高頻率、標準化的循環運轉動線。[ THE STRATEGY ] On-site logistics was broken down into precise geometry. Four dedicated vehicles were deployed: one designated as the "VVIP shuttle" — exclusively assigned to Twitch leadership and core partner executives during the prime window, escorting top stakeholders with an unbroken experience; the other three ran on a high-frequency, standardized loop.

【影響與交付 // THE IMPACT】 在 2 小時的極限壓力下，流暢交付 80 組以上頂級實況主與貴賓的接駁訪問。寫下單車每小時運轉 10 趟的極限效率，將高風險公共場域收斂為流暢、可控的品牌體驗觸點。[ THE IMPACT ] Within a two-hour pressure window, 80+ premier streamer and VIP transfers were delivered seamlessly. Each vehicle sustained a peak rate of 10 trips per hour — converting a high-risk public arena into a well-controlled, fluid brand touchpoint.

【挑戰 // THE CONSTRAINT】 在預算高度受限的條件下，需要打破單一場域的限制，在多線並行的環境中籌辦覆蓋全台北汎德展間的新車上市，同時克服跨場域名單碎片化與即時追蹤的瓶頸。[ THE CONSTRAINT ] Under a severely limited budget, the single-venue constraint had to be broken — staging a multi-timeline, multi-track new-car launch across all Pan Germany dealerships in Taipei, while overcoming the lead-list fragmentation and real-time tracking bottlenecks created by parallel events.

【戰略與行動 // THE STRATEGY】 以商業回報為終點逆向規劃。大膽引入外部品牌贊助以極大化槓桿商業資源；首夜以「濱江發表晚會」定調品牌高度，次日迅速連動其餘三處展示中心推行「邀請制發表活動」，形成高密度的轉換矩陣。同步設計目標導向的動態數據回報流，理順跨展間的數據痛點。[ THE STRATEGY ] Reverse-engineered from the commercial outcome. External brand sponsorship was brought in to maximize resource leverage; opening night was anchored with the Binjiang Gala to set the brand altitude, then the remaining three dealerships activated the next day via invitation-only launches — forming a dense conversion matrix.
